# ACI 209R-92 creep model documentation

This application computes the creep coefficient `φ(t,t0)` of moist-cured or steam-cured concrete using ACI 209R-92. The input time `t` is concrete age measured from casting; creep duration is `t - t0`.

ACI now identifies the report as `ACI PRC-209-92`; engineering literature and this application retain the more familiar name `ACI 209R-92`. [Source: ACI official product page][2]

## Model scope

ACI 209R-92 discusses creep, shrinkage, temperature effects, and the age development of strength and elastic modulus. This page implements only its **creep-coefficient** path, not shrinkage, strength development, elastic modulus, aging coefficients, or structural response. [Source: official ACI 209R-92 description][1]

The model scope and conditions in ACI 209.2R-08 include: [Source: ACI 209.2R-08 §1.2][3]

- Prediction of compressive creep and shrinkage in hardened concrete; the predictions may be assumed applicable to tension and shear.
- Concrete moist-cured for at least 1 day and loaded at or after the end of curing.
- A 28-day mean cylinder compressive-strength range of at least **20–70 MPa (3000–10000 psi)**.
- Calibration for concrete of typical composition. Concrete containing silica fume, more than 30% fly ash, or natural pozzolans requires calibration against tests of the actual materials.
- For structures sensitive to creep and shrinkage, ACI recommends calibration against tests of the actual concrete rather than reliance on empirical predictions alone.

## Input parameters

| Interface meaning | Batch column | Unit | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Curing method | `curingType` | - | `moist` (moist curing) or `steam` (steam curing) |
| Loading age | `t0` | d | Concrete age at first loading |
| Relative humidity | `H` | % | Ambient relative humidity |
| Volume-to-surface ratio | `VS` | mm | Member volume divided by exposed surface area |
| Slump | `slump` | mm | Slump of fresh concrete |
| Fine-aggregate percentage | `fineAggregate` | % | Fine aggregate as a mass percentage of total aggregate |
| Air content | `airContent` | % | Air content of concrete |
| Calculation age | `t` | d | Target age measured from casting |

When `t <= t0`, creep has not started and the application returns `φ = 0`.

## Equations

ACI 209R-92 Eq. (2-8) uses elapsed time after loading to calculate creep development: [Source: ACI 209R-92 §2.4, Eq. (2-8)][1]

```math
\phi(t,t_0)=\frac{(t-t_0)^{0.6}}{10+(t-t_0)^{0.6}}\phi_u
```

The ultimate creep coefficient uses a baseline value of 2.35 multiplied by correction factors for loading age, humidity, member size, slump, fine aggregate, and air content: [Sources: ACI 209R-92 §2.5–2.6; ACI 209.2R-08 Appendix A.1][1][3]

```math
\phi_u=2.35\gamma_{la}\gamma_{RH}\gamma_{V/S}\gamma_s\gamma_\psi\gamma_\alpha
```

### Loading-age correction

```math
\gamma_{la}=\begin{cases}
1, & \text{moist curing and }t_0\le7\text{ d}\\
1.25t_0^{-0.118}, & \text{moist curing and }t_0>7\text{ d}\\
1, & \text{steam curing and }t_0\le3\text{ d}\\
1.13t_0^{-0.094}, & \text{steam curing and }t_0>3\text{ d}
\end{cases}
```

### Relative-humidity correction

```math
\gamma_{RH}=\begin{cases}
1, & H\le40\%\\
1.27-0.0067H, & H>40\%
\end{cases}
```

### Member-size correction

`VS` is the volume-to-exposed-surface ratio, in mm:

```math
\gamma_{V/S}=\frac{2}{3}\left[1+1.13e^{-0.0213(V/S)}\right]
```

### Concrete-composition corrections

Slump is in mm; fine aggregate and air content are percentages: [Sources: ACI 209R-92 §2.6; ACI 209.2R-08 Appendix A.1][1][3]

```math
\gamma_s=0.82+0.00264s_{mm}
```

```math
\gamma_\psi=0.88+0.0024\psi_{\%}
```

```math
\gamma_\alpha=\max\left(1,0.46+0.09\alpha_{\%}\right)
```

The cement-content correction belongs to the ACI 209R-92 **shrinkage model** and does not enter the creep-coefficient calculation above.

## Output definition

| Output | Unit | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `φ(t,t0)` | - | Ratio of creep strain to the initial strain at loading |

Single-point calculation generates a concrete-age curve from 0 to 10000 days; batch calculation returns one result for each row's `t`.

## Limitations

- ACI classifies these documents as committee reports/guides, not mandatory specification provisions suitable for direct incorporation into contracts. The responsible engineer must rewrite any contractual requirements in mandatory language. [Source: introductory statement of ACI 209.2R-08][3]
- Creep tests and predictions show considerable scatter. ACI 209.2R-08 explicitly notes that differences between creep predictions and tests may exceed those for shrinkage. Critical structures require material testing and extrapolation of long-term behavior based on those results. [Source: ACI 209.2R-08 §1.1][3]
- This calculator provides empirical model predictions, not a substitute for material testing, project-specific analysis, or the judgment of a licensed engineer.
- This page does not calculate creep strain itself. Creep strain also requires the loading stress and the elastic modulus at loading, with consistent units.
